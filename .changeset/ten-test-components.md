Add ten customizable components: SpinningMark (`nami-motion/brand`), BlurText, ImageGenerating, VideoGenerating and MatrixOrb (`nami-motion/skia`), StreamingText (`nami-motion/text`), StatusRow, ThinkingBlock and QuestionCard (`nami-motion/chat`), SwipeActions (`nami-motion/gestures`) and BarList, Meter, Sparkline, RingGauge and CountUp (`nami-motion/charts`). Every component takes its icon, font, colors and timing as props and settles to a static state when reduce motion is on.
